Heat pump service

Every heat pump service ends with a full written report you can keep, covering the checks, readings and anything we recommend.

  • Electrical connections and isolators
  • Strainer and magnetic filter cleaned
  • Antifreeze protection tested
  • Expansion vessels checked and charged
  • Unvented cylinder safety checks
  • Flow rates, settings and software
  • Compressor hours and performance logged
  • Pipework insulation checked

Air conditioning service

A clean, well-maintained system cools faster, costs less to run and keeps the air in the room fresh.

  • Filters cleaned or replaced
  • Indoor coil and fan cleaned
  • Condensate drain checked and cleared
  • Outdoor unit cleaned and inspected
  • Electrical connections checked
  • Heating and cooling performance tested
