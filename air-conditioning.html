Cooling that heats too

Modern air conditioning works as an air-to-air heat pump. The same unit that cools a bedroom in July can heat it in January, usually for less than an electric heater.

Air-to-air systems can now qualify for a £2,500 government grant when they replace fossil fuel heating. We'll tell you at the survey if yours does.

Common questions

Anything else, just call us on 01623 431028.

Can air conditioning heat as well as cool?

Yes. Almost all the systems we fit are air-to-air heat pumps, so they heat as well as cool and switch between the two automatically.

Is it noisy?

Indoor units are designed to be very quiet, and outdoor units are positioned to keep noise away from you and your neighbours. We'll talk through the best spot at the survey.

How long does an install take?

A single wall unit is often fitted in a day. Multi-room systems and commercial jobs take longer, and we'll give you a timescale with your quote.

Does air conditioning need servicing?

Yes. A yearly service keeps it efficient, keeps the filters and coils clean, and catches small problems before they turn into breakdowns.
