Why a heat pump

A heat pump takes warmth from the outside air, even in freezing weather, and uses it to heat your radiators or underfloor heating and your hot water.

Designed and set up properly, it gives steady, comfortable heat with no gas, no oil deliveries and no flue. Getting the design right is what makes it efficient, which is why we size every system to the house.

Common questions

Ask us anything on 01623 431028.

Do heat pumps work in winter?

Yes. Air source heat pumps are designed to keep working well below freezing. The key is a system sized for your home's heat loss, which is what our survey works out.

Will I need new radiators?

Sometimes. Heat pumps run at lower water temperatures than boilers, so some rooms may need a larger radiator. The heat loss survey shows exactly which ones, if any.

What about hot water?

The heat pump heats a hot water cylinder. If you have a combi boiler now, we'll find a good spot for the cylinder as part of the design.

I'm off the gas grid. Is it worth it?

Often, yes. Homes on oil or LPG with no mains gas can get £9,000 off until March 2027, and you'll no longer need fuel deliveries.

Does a heat pump need servicing?

Yes. An annual service checks the electrics, water quality, antifreeze, pressures and settings, and keeps your manufacturer's warranty valid.
