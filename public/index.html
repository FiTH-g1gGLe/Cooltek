Government grants towards your new system

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme takes money off the cost of replacing fossil fuel heating. Homes heated by oil or LPG with no mains gas can get £9,000 towards a heat pump until March 2027.

The grant is claimed by your MCS installer, so we apply for it and take it straight off your quote.

Figures from the government's Boiler Upgrade Scheme for England and Wales, checked November 2026. Eligibility rules apply. We'll confirm what your property qualifies for at the survey.
